Invention Party

The pass-and-play party game where two random objects collide and your job is to invent something gloriously ridiculous. Each round, everyone sees the same two objects and mashes them into one brilliant (or absurd) invention — type your pitch or doodle it right on the screen. Then the judge ranks the best ideas, the judge role rotates, and the highest score wins. One phone, everyone plays, no internet required.
