Privacy Policy — Invention Party

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Soy Sauce Labs ("we," "us") built Invention Party to be a fun, private party game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the game runs on your device, and we do not collect personal information about you.

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect, store, or sell personal information. Invention Party has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Data that stays on your device

  • Gameplay. Player names, scores, settings, and game progress are stored only on your device and are used only to run the game.
  • Custom photo cards. If you create custom cards from your photos, those images are used only within your game session on your device. They are not uploaded to us or shared with anyone.
  • Purchases. Records of what you've unlocked are stored on your device so your content stays available.

Photo library access

If you choose to make a custom photo card, the app asks the system photo picker for the specific image you select. The app only receives the photo you pick — it does not read or scan your photo library.

AI deck generation

Invention Party includes an optional feature that generates a themed deck of cards. If you use it, the theme text you type is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) solely to generate the card list and artwork for that deck, and the results are returned to your device. Please avoid entering personal or sensitive information as a theme. If you never use this feature, the app makes no such requests. OpenAI's handling of that request is governed by its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Any purchases (deck packs, subscriptions, or AI credits) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of payments is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Invention Party is family-friendly and safe for children. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the revised version here with a new "Last updated" date.
