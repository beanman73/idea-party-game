Frequently asked questions

How do you play Invention Party?

Add your players, pick an object pack, and start a round. Everyone sees the same two random objects and invents something by combining them — you can type your idea or draw it right on the screen. The judge ranks the best inventions, points are awarded, the judge role rotates, and the highest score after the final round wins.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game and all built-in packs work fully offline on a single device. An internet connection is only needed if you choose to generate a custom AI deck.

How many people can play?

It's a pass-and-play party game for a group on one device. There's also a Solo Mode for quick practice.

I paid for a pack or credits but don't see them.

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap “Restore Purchases.” If it still doesn't appear, email us at the address above with the Apple ID email used for the purchase and we'll help sort it out.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. Invention Party is designed to be family-friendly. It contains no ads and no third-party tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

Please email us — real feedback from players is how the game gets better.
